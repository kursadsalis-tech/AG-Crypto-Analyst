# AGCA PAPER Execution Gate Register

This register is binding for the active behavior-based PAPER path. A gate may
not silently move between categories. New hard vetoes require explicit evidence,
a regression test, and an explanation of why sizing cannot contain the risk.

| Gate | Category | Current effect | Product judgment |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAPER_ONLY / real-order authority | Real-capital safety | Real orders impossible | Permanent until separate user approval and evidence gates |
| Corrupt, missing, future or stale evidence | Evidence integrity | Hard veto | Required; fabricated or unavailable evidence cannot form a trade |
| Missing dynamic asset risk record | Evidence integrity | Hard veto plus missed-opportunity record | Required until valid risk measurements exist |
| Unexecutable liquidity / exchange minimum | Execution feasibility | Hard veto | Required; a fill that could not execute is not a PAPER trade |
| Missing executable market route | Execution feasibility | Hard veto/cancellation when the selected Binance Spot or USD-M route lacks a fresh executable quote/liquidity path | Sizing cannot repair a missing executable market route; the agent may not silently substitute another exchange or market |
| Volatility, range, drawdown, recent direction | Position sizing | Reduces cap; valid liquid extremes receive a small cap | Must never become a context-blind PAPER asset veto |
| Global helper uncertainty | Portfolio sizing | Fixed cohort or cross-source helper failure produces visible DEFENSIVE-or-tighter caps and at most one new position; canonical candidate risk/execution evidence must still be valid | No fabricated reference data, no context-blind global veto for helper outage; actual current total/alt/single/stop-risk caps bind selection and fills |
| Global market tail stress | Portfolio sizing | At most 2% total, 1% alt, 0.25% per position and one new position | Valid data under stress remains learnable; only `DATA_BLOCKED` stops all PAPER entries |
| Five closed samples, one UTC day, positive cost-after mean | Hypothesis formation | Allows small exploration, not promotion | Not a safety claim; must be reviewed from accumulated PAPER outcomes |
| 30 observed samples plus 10 own closed PAPER trades across three days, 55% calibrated wins, +0.05% calibrated mean edge | Promotion evidence | Only changes trusted/promoted status and size | Own cost-after execution must confirm observational evidence; this must never block the smaller exploration path |
| Own closed PAPER execution feedback | Adaptive ranking, sizing and evidence-mature suspension | Bayesian-shrunk cost-after results rerank matching event/pattern/direction candidates and adjust the next experiment size within 0.5x-1.25x. A small losing sample reduces exposure while calibrated net edge remains positive. Non-finite or non-positive calibrated edge cannot enter normal execution or an unbudgeted negative-edge experiment. Once at least 10 own closed PAPER trades span at least three independent days and the calibrated net edge is <=0, that matching pattern remains visibly suspended. A separate recovery hypothesis may use at least five new, closed observations after the last own execution, from the latest 72 hours (maximum 120 samples); it keeps the same pattern/direction execution feedback, must regain positive calibrated edge, cannot promote, and uses a quarter-normal soft size target, raised to the 100 USDT actual OPEN floor only within hard caps, with at most 1.5% equity gross exposure | User-authorized 2026-09-30 correction: sizing cannot turn negative expected return positive; negative-edge execution is therefore disabled rather than granted an implicit unlimited exploration budget. A small losing sample alone remains insufficient for mature suspension. Recovery is earned by new evidence, never a forced direction flip or a reset of loss history. Future, open, ineligible or non-PAPER outcomes cannot enter calibration |
| Single realtime PAPER lifecycle | Runtime availability | `agca-paper-realtime-worker.service` is the only deployable PAPER lifecycle authority. It consumes fresh Binance states continuously, scans completed causal opportunity evidence on a bounded cadence, fills only valid pending plans, manages OPEN positions and triggers outcome review without a parallel selection timer | No second worker/timer may select, fill or manage the same portfolio. Support timers may prepare evidence/risk/technical context only and cannot create trades |
| Book/taker relationship | Learnable market context | Aligned and opposing/absorption patterns can both be learned | Opposing signs are not automatically discarded |
| Minimum PAPER notional | Execution feasibility | Actual new OPEN notional must be at least 100 USDT; 100 is a floor, not a ceiling. Normal/recovery soft targets include rounding headroom; cash, exposure, asset, stop-risk and observed liquidity remain hard caps | User decision 2026-09-30. Partial or rounded fills below 100 are cancelled with an audited reason before cash/exposure mutation. Replans below the floor cancel. Reduce-only exits are exempt, including dust |
| Deep analysis capacity | Scheduling | Broad radar membership and fresh per-symbol behavior remain independent of the deep shortlist. The observer uses at most 10 deep streams (current CLI default; service has no slot override); the shared orchestrator plans at most 12 candidates per scan and records excess as `DEEP_ANALYSIS_CAPACITY_DEFERRED`, rotating deferred opportunities | A slot limit is temporary capacity, never a new asset veto or trade authority; missing fresh evidence/risk/technical plans still fails closed |
| Six open positions / three new per cycle / 60% exposure | Portfolio sizing | Defers additional candidates | Capacity control, not a statement that the opportunity is invalid |
| Existing active symbol / already selected decision | Idempotency | Does not duplicate exposure or thesis | Required for one-agent, one-portfolio accounting |
| Pending-entry market route | Execution availability | Every still-valid `PENDING_ENTRY` symbol/market pair remains in the realtime subscription universe until it fills or is cancelled. A 1x LONG routed to SPOT must therefore receive its own Spot executable quote even when its opportunity evidence originated from a perpetual-swap behavior feed. Pending intents older than the bounded executable observation window are cancelled before subscription refresh even if no market callback ever arrived | A selected venue may not be silently stranded or replaced merely because the discovery feed used another market type, and an unreachable route may not remain a zombie subscription indefinitely |
| Runtime source identity | Deployment integrity | The realtime worker captures its own git SHA/dirty state once at process start and writes that process-bound identity into health; cloud attestation treats that process identity as authoritative and also records the current checkout identity separately | A checkout updated after a long-lived worker started must not masquerade as proof that the running process loaded the new code |
| Canonical market-data provider | Evidence integrity | Active PAPER discovery, microstructure, funding, liquidation observation, realtime quotes and executable liquidity use Binance. The market-behavior observer is WebSocket-only: all-market ticker for broad discovery, deep depth/trade/mark-price streams for selected symbols, and a liquidation stream. Behavior-minute rows carry `market_data_provider='BINANCE'`; legacy/other-provider rows are excluded from decision, risk-universe and realtime-subscription queries. Dynamic historical risk context uses explicitly labelled same-exchange Binance Spot history when Futures REST is unavailable; symbols without valid same-exchange history fail closed at the asset level | A trade thesis must not blend exchange-specific microstructure as if it were one market. WebSocket-unavailable capabilities remain explicitly unavailable rather than being fabricated or silently sourced elsewhere; raw high-frequency payloads remain memory/local only |
| Risk snapshot freshness | Risk integrity | Global/full-history risk remains on the isolated 15-minute support timer. Missing dynamic symbols receive bounded local incremental coverage every 15 seconds (12 symbols, two-second budget), using 360 exact closed canonical Binance 1m bars with explicit six-hour scope and at most 1.5% equity asset cap; missing, corrupt or insufficient-liquidity data still fails closed. Behavior decisions fail closed when the latest risk policy is older than 30 minutes or timestamp-invalid | A stale stop/cap snapshot may never remain actionable merely because it is the newest row in SQLite; the support timer is not a second trade authority |
| Risk-support SQLite contention | Runtime reliability | The risk support connection waits up to 30 seconds for transient SQLite writer contention before treating an audit/snapshot write as failed | Genuine source-validation failures still fail closed, but a short local audit-write collision must not be mislabeled as a BTC cross-source data failure |
| Adaptive technical execution plan | Decision and execution geometry | Every new behavior/PRICE_ACTION PAPER candidate is only an opportunity trigger. Before selection it must have a fresh network-free technical plan built from the local canonical Binance USD-M closed-1m cache plus fresh higher-timeframe context. The plan adaptively selects relevant frames, affects ranking/sizing softly, freezes an entry zone, exact structural+ATR invalidation stop and structural TP1/TP2, and is stored in the immutable thesis | No single indicator may veto direction. Missing/stale technical data or a structural stop outside the 0.5%-5% execution envelope is an execution-feasibility veto. SPOT execution freezes one contemporaneous Spot/USD-M basis ratio; later ticks may not move the plan. A stale plan may not fill |
| Technical bar cache | Evidence integrity and latency | Broad eligible Binance symbols publish exact closed `kline_1m` bars into a bounded local-only SQLite cache. Missing initial history is checksum-verified from the same Binance USD-M Vision archive in the off-hot-path support cycle; 5m/15m and other intraday frames are locally aggregated | Candidate evaluation performs no network request. Raw high-frequency frames are not mirrored, the cache is bounded, and another exchange may not fill history gaps |
| Adaptive long-regime context | Decision context | The support cycle may prepare closed 1d history and causal completed 3d/1w/1M regime bars for currently relevant symbols. The pre-trade planner requests these frames only for long-horizon or macro-like setups; ordinary fast setups do not run every timeframe | The agent chooses what it needs rather than blindly consulting every frame. If a setup explicitly requires a long regime that is unavailable, that candidate fails closed instead of inventing context |
| Historical technical-shadow review | Legacy learning evidence only | The existing review remains valid only for historical theses where technical context was explicitly observation-only. New authoritative technical-plan trades are excluded from that shadow cohort and are evaluated through their frozen execution-plan evidence | Historical association evidence may not be reinterpreted as proof of the new authoritative plan; forward economics of new plan trades must be tracked separately |
| Adverse same-setup re-entry | Churn control and learning integrity | After `PROTECTIVE_STOP`, `THESIS_INVALIDATED` or PAPER liquidation, an identical symbol/direction/source/scope/event-kind/pattern/source-set fingerprint cannot reopen merely because a new decision id/minute exists. Re-entry is allowed immediately if the material evidence fingerprint changes, or once price has reset by at least one current bounded stop-risk unit (1R) from the prior exit | No fixed time cooldown: fast genuine market change remains actionable, while repeated funding of the same failed thesis is blocked |
| Immediate causal PAPER entry | Execution realism | The realtime worker attempts a fill only from the fresh executable side of the selected Binance Spot or USD-M route and only while price is inside the frozen technical entry zone. Stale/missing quote, visible liquidity, stale plan or invalid cross-market basis fails closed | The agent may act immediately when price is already acceptable, but it must not chase outside the planned zone. Pending intent remains bounded and a new market tick cannot silently re-anchor the frozen plan |
| Route metadata and frozen execution rules | Failure isolation | Fresh validated metadata is required independently for each route's new entries. Actual fill rules are frozen in immutable hashed ENTRY_FILLED evidence. OPEN stops/liquidation/reduce-only exits use frozen rules after helper loss. Existing positions may adopt current validated rules in an explicitly labelled immutable adoption event | No stale metadata authorizes new exposure. Legacy rules are never claimed as original fill evidence; unavailable/corrupt evidence remains visible and fails closed. Direct official refresh is primary, optional bounded Supabase egress is fallback |
| Behavior pattern cache | Producer integrity | Exact V2 contract, producer/core equality and complete schema validation; source SHA/process identity visible | Mismatch blocks new candidates only; never OPEN management |
| Runtime acceptance | Deployment integrity | Fresh health, exact process SHA, clean tracked checkout, compatible versions, stable PID and zero NRestarts delta over 60 seconds; metadata/cache preflight before consumer restart | An active unit alone is insufficient. Four starts per 300 seconds, 30-second restart interval bound crash storms |
| Real-time open-position management | Execution and loss containment | Every OPEN position is managed from the dedicated Binance WebSocket route for its selected market. New technical-plan positions carry frozen structural TP1/TP2 and exact protective stop; legacy 1R/2R targets remain fallback only when no technical targets exist. Fresh executable quote/depth and trade-flow evidence drive PAPER management; stale events, duplicate timestamps and missing visible liquidity fail closed | The former unconditional horizon wait is forbidden. Horizon is only a maximum timeout; structural stop, thesis invalidation, staged technical profit taking and trailing stop may act earlier |
| Flow thesis confirmation | Outcome-blind thesis validity | Only a frozen FLOW_POS LONG or FLOW_NEG SHORT continuation thesis can be contradicted by flow sign. Both book and taker must oppose by at least 0.20 in three fresh advancing observations spaced at least five seconds; neutral/missing/stale flow or a gap over ten seconds resets persistence. Reversal/absorption entry flow is not itself invalidation | This measures contradictory evidence persistence, not minimum position age. Structural stop, liquidation and horizon timeout remain independently actionable |
| Protective stop | Loss containment | New technical-plan positions initialize the exact frozen structure+ATR invalidation level after the actual fill, with 0.5%-5% stop geometry. Legacy positions retain the bounded risk-snapshot stop fallback | Stop, sizing risk and immutable thesis must describe the same plan. Missing/invalid technical geometry for a new authoritative-plan position is fail-closed; no default technical stop may be invented |
| Funding reconciliation | Accounting integrity | Exit execution never waits for the funding transport. Exact exposed interval and quantity are frozen, the position is temporarily excluded from learning, and funding is reconciled asynchronously before outcome review | A reporting dependency may not delay a protective exit, but missing cost may not silently become zero |
| Position event audit | Learning integrity | Stop initialization/change, opposing thesis evidence, partial realization, rejected exit and final close are immutable local SQLite events with source hash and `real_order_allowed=0` | Replayed or older market events cannot mutate cash twice |
| External causal event match | Strategy context | Event-linked setups remain available but are not mandatory | Endogenous `MARKET_STRUCTURE` samples and trades cover order-flow opportunities without reconnecting Unified Forecast |
| Independent signal routes | Learnable strategy context | A cost-positive, point-in-time PRICE_ACTION, MARKET_BEHAVIOR/order-flow, or causal external event hypothesis may open a small PAPER experiment by itself | Confluence is a sizing input, never a requirement that chart, news and book must all agree before learning can occur |
| PAPER leverage tier | Position sizing and risk learning | Outcome-blind independent directional sources plus promotion evidence select 1x-5x; one weak valid source starts at 1x and a soft 0.5% equity margin target, raised to the actual OPEN floor only when hard limits permit, while five steps cap at 5x and 2% equity margin | Explicitly approved by the user on 2026-09-02. Leverage is PAPER-only; duplicate/opposing evidence cannot inflate it, gross exposure remains inside asset and 60% portfolio caps |
| Margin and gross exposure accounting | Accounting integrity | Cash reserves isolated margin plus entry fee; quantity, fees, spread, slippage and funding use gross exposure; close returns remaining margin plus P&L after costs | A leverage label without separate margin/exposure accounting is forbidden |
| Venue-specific PAPER fee contract | Execution economics | Selection, risk sizing, cash reservation, preselection and simulated fills use one shared taker-fee function keyed by the selected market route. Defaults are independently configurable for SPOT and USD-M; venue-specific configuration overrides legacy generic fee settings | The same PAPER trade may not be evaluated with one fee assumption and booked with another. Funding remains a separate perpetual-only cost |
| Leveraged liquidation | Loss containment and learning | Each leveraged OPEN has an immutable maintenance-margin liquidation threshold; a fresh price crossing it closes at the threshold, charges the exchange liquidation fee and records the loss | Protective stops normally precede liquidation, but a gap may liquidate and must remain visible learning evidence rather than being hidden |

Realtime portfolio valuation uses the same fresh, exact-market Binance bid/ask
contract as PAPER fills: long holdings are marked at bid and shorts at ask.
Missing, stale, future or crossed quotes fail closed. Dynamic Spot holdings do
not require a research-universe hourly candle. Legacy offline reports retain
the existing closed-candle valuation path; this adds no order authority.

Every selection cycle records its core status, candidate count, selected count,
capacity, available exposure, and rejection reason counts locally in
`paper_behavior_selection_runs`. Core causes survive orchestration: no learned
pattern, suspension, non-positive calibrated edge, unavailable event/evidence,
risk coverage, technical rejection and capacity are distinct. Direction telemetry
records LONG/SHORT pattern, suspended, exploration, recovery, candidate and selected
counts plus the last 20 persisted positions, explicitly a bounded cohort. A zero-trade cycle without an explicit recorded
cause is an operational failure, not a healthy outcome.

Canonical timestamps remain UTC for deterministic joins and audit. Every
operator-facing PAPER time must render Istanbul time first and include UTC in
parentheses; an unlabeled UTC timestamp is not an acceptable user display.

Unified Forecast is baseline-only. This register does not authorize a second
agent, a second portfolio, automatic policy promotion, capital changes, or real
orders. The 1x-5x authority applies only to simulated isolated-margin PAPER
positions.
