# AGCA PAPER Execution Gate Register

This register is binding for the active behavior-based PAPER path. A gate may
not silently move between categories. New hard vetoes require explicit evidence,
a regression test, and an explanation of why sizing cannot contain the risk.

| Gate | Category | Current effect | Product judgment |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAPER_ONLY / real-order authority | Real-capital safety | Real orders impossible | Permanent until separate user approval and evidence gates |
| Corrupt, missing, future or stale evidence | Evidence integrity | Hard veto | Required; fabricated or unavailable evidence cannot form a trade |
| Missing dynamic asset risk record | Evidence integrity | Hard veto plus missed-opportunity record | Required until valid risk measurements exist |
| Unexecutable liquidity / exchange minimum | Execution feasibility | Hard veto | Required; a fill that could not execute is not a PAPER trade |
| Missing executable market route | Execution feasibility | Hard veto/cancellation when the selected Binance Spot or USD-M route lacks a fresh executable quote/liquidity path | Sizing cannot repair a missing executable market route; the agent may not silently substitute another exchange or market |
| Volatility, range, drawdown, recent direction | Position sizing | Reduces cap; valid liquid extremes receive a small cap | Must never become a context-blind PAPER asset veto |
| Global market tail stress | Portfolio sizing | At most 2% total, 1% alt, 0.25% per position and one new position | Valid data under stress remains learnable; only `DATA_BLOCKED` stops all PAPER entries |
| Five closed samples, one UTC day, positive cost-after mean | Hypothesis formation | Allows small exploration, not promotion | Not a safety claim; must be reviewed from accumulated PAPER outcomes |
| 30 observed samples plus 10 own closed PAPER trades across three days, 55% calibrated wins, +0.05% calibrated mean edge | Promotion evidence | Only changes trusted/promoted status and size | Own cost-after execution must confirm observational evidence; this must never block the smaller exploration path |
| Own closed PAPER execution feedback | Adaptive ranking, sizing and evidence-mature suspension | Bayesian-shrunk cost-after results rerank matching event/pattern/direction candidates and adjust the next experiment size within 0.5x-1.25x. A small losing sample only reduces exposure. Once at least 10 own closed PAPER trades span at least three independent days and the calibrated net edge is <=0, that matching pattern is suspended from new PAPER exposure while its evidence remains available for review/challengers | Sizing may absorb early noise, but it must not keep funding a mature negative expectancy. Future, open, ineligible or non-PAPER outcomes cannot enter calibration |
| Single realtime PAPER lifecycle | Runtime availability | `runtime.paper_realtime_worker` is the only deployable selection/fill/management lifecycle authority. Opportunity selection runs off the websocket event loop in its decision executor; support/risk timers never own the trade lifecycle | There must be one portfolio and one lifecycle authority. Support collectors may prepare evidence but cannot open, fill, manage or close trades |
| Book/taker relationship | Learnable market context | Aligned and opposing/absorption patterns can both be learned | Opposing signs are not automatically discarded |
| Minimum PAPER notional | Execution feasibility | Fixed 10 USDT floor | The former 1% equity floor was an invalid hidden learning veto and is removed |
| Six open positions / three new per cycle / 60% exposure | Portfolio sizing | Defers additional candidates | Capacity control, not a statement that the opportunity is invalid |
| Existing active symbol / already selected decision | Idempotency | Does not duplicate exposure or thesis | Required for one-agent, one-portfolio accounting |
| Pending-entry market route and technical zone | Execution availability | Every still-valid `PENDING_ENTRY` symbol/market pair remains in the realtime subscription universe until fill/cancel. New technical-plan entries fill only when the fresh executable side and simulated fill remain inside the frozen entry zone; a stale/invalid plan is cancelled before generic pending expiry | A selected route may not be silently stranded or replaced, and the agent may not chase a setup after price has left the technical entry geometry. Legacy pre-plan positions remain manageable rather than being orphaned |
| Runtime source identity | Deployment integrity | The realtime worker captures its own git SHA/dirty state once at process start and writes that process-bound identity into health; cloud attestation treats that process identity as authoritative and also records the current checkout identity separately | A checkout updated after a long-lived worker started must not masquerade as proof that the running process loaded the new code |
| Canonical market-data provider | Evidence integrity | Active PAPER discovery, microstructure, funding, liquidation observation, realtime quotes and executable liquidity use Binance. The market-behavior observer is WebSocket-only: all-market ticker for broad discovery, deep depth/trade/mark-price streams for selected symbols, and a liquidation stream. Behavior-minute rows carry `market_data_provider='BINANCE'`; legacy/other-provider rows are excluded from decision, risk-universe and realtime-subscription queries. Dynamic historical risk context uses explicitly labelled same-exchange Binance Spot history when Futures REST is unavailable; symbols without valid same-exchange history fail closed at the asset level | A trade thesis must not blend exchange-specific microstructure as if it were one market. WebSocket-unavailable capabilities remain explicitly unavailable rather than being fabricated or silently sourced elsewhere; raw high-frequency payloads remain memory/local only |
| Risk snapshot freshness | Risk integrity | Market/asset risk is refreshed by an isolated PAPER-only support timer no more frequently than every 15 minutes. Behavior decisions fail closed when the latest risk policy is older than 30 minutes or timestamp-invalid | A stale stop/cap snapshot may never remain actionable merely because it is the newest row in SQLite; the support timer is not a second trade authority |
| Risk-support SQLite contention | Runtime reliability | The risk support connection waits up to 30 seconds for transient SQLite writer contention before treating an audit/snapshot write as failed | Genuine source-validation failures still fail closed, but a short local audit-write collision must not be mislabeled as a BTC cross-source data failure |
| Adaptive technical support context | Pre-trade decision support | The isolated risk/support cycle prepares fresh closed 1h/4h structure and, when same-exchange daily history is available, 1d/3d/1w/1M regime context. Binance observer snapshots also produce compact local closed-minute OHLC/aggressive-flow rows from which 1m/5m/15m frames are derived without a decision-time network call | Support preparation is not a second trade authority. The trade-plan layer selects only useful available timeframes for the setup; it must not fetch the network from the realtime decision hot path or pretend an unavailable timeframe exists |
| Authoritative technical trade plan | Pre-trade execution geometry | Every newly selected behavior/price-action/external-event PAPER candidate requires a fresh local technical plan before position creation. The plan may affect ranking and bounded sizing, freezes an entry zone, structure+volatility invalidation stop and structural TP1/TP2, and is captured in the immutable thesis. The trigger direction is not automatically overridden and no single indicator is a hard veto | Opportunity detection alone is not an entry command. Missing/stale technical support blocks only new PAPER exposure. A structural stop outside the 0.5%-5% risk envelope is execution-infeasible and is rejected rather than silently moved inward |
| Legacy technical-shadow forward review | Historical learning evidence only | The existing shadow review remains valid only for earlier frozen contexts that explicitly carried no technical decision authority. Authoritative technical-plan trades are excluded from that legacy shadow cohort and remain auditable through their frozen thesis and normal net-after-cost learning ledger | Historical shadow evidence must not be mixed with the new authoritative policy cohort or used to claim causation. Future technical-plan calibration may adjust bounded weights from forward economics, but does not grant real-order authority |
| Adverse same-setup re-entry | Churn control and learning integrity | After `PROTECTIVE_STOP`, `THESIS_INVALIDATED` or PAPER liquidation, an identical symbol/direction/source/scope/event-kind/pattern/source-set fingerprint cannot reopen merely because a new decision id/minute exists. Re-entry is allowed immediately if the material evidence fingerprint changes, or once price has reset by at least one current bounded stop-risk unit (1R) from the prior exit | No fixed time cooldown: fast genuine market change remains actionable, while repeated funding of the same failed thesis is blocked |
| Immediate causal PAPER entry | Execution realism | Once a fresh candidate has passed the technical plan and its executable quote is inside the frozen entry zone, the realtime worker attempts the PAPER fill from the fresh Binance side; stale/missing quote or visible liquidity fails closed | Waiting for the next hourly open is forbidden, but so is blindly chasing the trigger. Entry timing is causal and bounded by both live executable evidence and the frozen technical geometry |
| Real-time open-position management | Execution and loss containment | Every OPEN position is managed from the dedicated Binance WebSocket route for its selected market. Fresh executable quote/depth and trade-flow evidence drive PAPER management; stale events, duplicate timestamps and missing visible liquidity fail closed | The former unconditional horizon wait is forbidden. Horizon is only a maximum timeout; protective stop, thesis invalidation, staged profit taking and trailing stop may act earlier |
| Protective stop | Loss containment | New technical-plan positions use the exact frozen structure+volatility invalidation level when its distance is within the 0.5%-5% risk envelope. Legacy positions retain their historical bounded volatility fallback | Missing/invalid technical geometry blocks new exposure; the manager may not invent or move a structural stop inward merely to make a candidate fit the risk budget |
| Funding reconciliation | Accounting integrity | Exit execution never waits for the funding transport. Exact exposed interval and quantity are frozen, the position is temporarily excluded from learning, and funding is reconciled asynchronously before outcome review | A reporting dependency may not delay a protective exit, but missing cost may not silently become zero |
| Position event audit | Learning integrity | Stop initialization/change, opposing thesis evidence, partial realization, rejected exit and final close are immutable local SQLite events with source hash and `real_order_allowed=0` | Replayed or older market events cannot mutate cash twice |
| External causal event match | Strategy context | Event-linked setups remain available but are not mandatory | Endogenous `MARKET_STRUCTURE` samples and trades cover order-flow opportunities without reconnecting Unified Forecast |
| Independent signal routes | Learnable strategy context | A cost-positive, point-in-time PRICE_ACTION, MARKET_BEHAVIOR/order-flow, or causal external event hypothesis may open a small PAPER experiment by itself | Confluence is a sizing input, never a requirement that chart, news and book must all agree before learning can occur |
| PAPER leverage tier | Position sizing and risk learning | Outcome-blind independent directional sources plus promotion evidence select 1x-5x; one weak valid source starts at 1x and 0.5% equity margin, while five steps cap at 5x and 2% equity margin | Explicitly approved by the user on 2026-09-02. Leverage is PAPER-only; duplicate/opposing evidence cannot inflate it, gross exposure remains inside asset and 60% portfolio caps |
| Margin and gross exposure accounting | Accounting integrity | Cash reserves isolated margin plus entry fee; quantity, fees, spread, slippage and funding use gross exposure; close returns remaining margin plus P&L after costs | A leverage label without separate margin/exposure accounting is forbidden |
| Venue-specific PAPER fee contract | Execution economics | Selection, risk sizing, cash reservation, preselection and simulated fills use one shared taker-fee function keyed by the selected market route. Defaults are independently configurable for SPOT and USD-M; venue-specific configuration overrides legacy generic fee settings | The same PAPER trade may not be evaluated with one fee assumption and booked with another. Funding remains a separate perpetual-only cost |
| Leveraged liquidation | Loss containment and learning | Each leveraged OPEN has an immutable maintenance-margin liquidation threshold; a fresh price crossing it closes at the threshold, charges the exchange liquidation fee and records the loss | Protective stops normally precede liquidation, but a gap may liquidate and must remain visible learning evidence rather than being hidden |

Every selection cycle records its core status, candidate count, selected count,
capacity, available exposure, and rejection reason counts locally in
`paper_behavior_selection_runs`. A zero-trade cycle without an explicit recorded
cause is an operational failure, not a healthy outcome.

Canonical timestamps remain UTC for deterministic joins and audit. Every
operator-facing PAPER time must render Istanbul time first and include UTC in
parentheses; an unlabeled UTC timestamp is not an acceptable user display.

Unified Forecast is baseline-only. This register does not authorize a second
agent, a second portfolio, automatic policy promotion, capital changes, or real
orders. The 1x-5x authority applies only to simulated isolated-margin PAPER
positions.
